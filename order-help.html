Your order, explained.

Confirmation, delivery updates and help in one place.

Your saved order receipt.

Checking checkout availability…

After payment, keep your private receipt link. It shows verified payment, the selected products and sizes, shipping charges, fulfillment status and available package tracking. Saving a bag or custom design does not place an order.

Automatic store confirmation and tracking emails are not connected. A Stripe payment receipt, when sent, confirms payment; it does not confirm shipment. Check your private receipt for fulfillment updates.

ConfirmationTrackingGet order help

Order confirmation.

Your private receipt confirms payment only after Stripe verifies it. Review the order number, selected items, sizes, colors, quantities and price breakdown there. Payment confirmation and Printful production approval are separate stages.

Missing confirmation? Check your spam folder and the email address used at checkout. Contact support before placing a duplicate order if you believe you were charged.

Help with a confirmation ↗

Follow each package.

Use Refresh order and tracking on your private receipt to retrieve the available carrier, tracking number and tracking link for each shipped package. Production time comes before transit time. A new label may appear before the carrier records its first scan.

Orders can arrive in separate packages. Each shipment needs its own tracking number and a list of the items it contains. A shipped package does not mean every item has shipped.

For a stalled update, missing package or delivery marked complete that you cannot find, include the carrier status and tracking number in your support message.
