Your order, explained.

Confirmation, delivery updates and help in one place.

Orders are not open yet.

Checkout is currently unavailable. This store cannot accept new orders, send order emails or look up live tracking yet. Saving a bag or a custom design does not place an order.

The guide below describes the order communication planned for launch.

ConfirmationTrackingGet order help

Order confirmation.

Once ordering opens, a confirmation email should arrive after successful payment and order acceptance. It will contain your order number, selected items, sizes, colors, quantities, price breakdown, delivery address and estimated timing.

Missing confirmation? Check your spam folder and the email address used at checkout. Contact support before placing a duplicate order if you believe you were charged.

Help with a confirmation ↗

Follow each package.

A shipping email is planned when your package ships, with its carrier, tracking number and tracking link. Production time comes before transit time. A new label may appear before the carrier records its first scan.

Orders can arrive in separate packages. Each shipment needs its own tracking number and a list of the items it contains. A shipped package does not mean every item has shipped.

For a stalled update, missing package or delivery marked complete that you cannot find, include the carrier status and tracking number in your support message.

Get order help.

Have your order number, the email used to order and a short description of the issue ready. For damaged items, include clear photos of the item, artwork and packaging in your email app.
