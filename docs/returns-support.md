# Returns and support
Prepared 2026-10-02, prelaunch. The site does not take orders. The owner supplied midnightdesign107@gmail.com on October 4, 2026; support-config.json publishes it for customer support and custom-design review. Do not invent a support@ address or publish a personal inbox inferred from repository metadata.

Contact is a local message composer, not a ticket service. Configuration makes mailto links available. The customer must press Send in their own email app and attach photos there. No server delivery, attachments, ticket ID, storage or response SLA is represented. A real incoming/outgoing email test is required to confirm the owner's inbox before launch; browser tests check the published address and use example.test fixtures without sending mail.

Fulfillment references:

https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50260990428177-What-photos-should-I-submit-when-reporting-a-problem-with-my-order

Customer policy is a retail policy, not a copy of the supplier reimbursement contract. Prompt 30-day reporting is requested but does not cut off mandatory consumer remedies. Wrong-size choice differs from a wrong size delivered or measurements outside tolerance. Supplier reimbursement and customer refunds are separate operations; an approved supplier wallet credit does not refund a retail customer.

Consumer rights reference:
https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm
Standard catalog goods are not automatically individualized because production follows an order. Before accepting orders in a market, complete the required seller identity, business/return address, withdrawal instructions and local consumer information. No return address is invented. Shipping, support and payment readiness must be confirmed before enabling checkout.
