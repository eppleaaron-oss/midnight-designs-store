07 / Before publication

Automatic pricing rules

Profit margin is profit divided by the discounted selling price, after confirmed costs and fees. Every assigned supplier variant must pass. Unknown costs, missing rules, or an impossible maximum price hold the design at the worker’s publication-readiness step. Zero extra charges must be verified explicitly. Prices are estimates from master-verified USD costs; destination taxes and any additional costs must be included where applicable. This does not change existing store prices or enable automatic publishing.

08 / Supplier and storefront

Product creation and publishing

Start in review mode. After a test product is published and you check its placement, resolution and mockups, mark its blueprint and supplier setup verified. Automatic mode requires those verified blueprints and their current category pricing rules. Changing back to review revokes pending automatic approvals; draft mode blocks new supplier and store writes.

After preparation and pricing checks, save the final listing and approve it.
