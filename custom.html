READY FOR YOUR NEXT PIECE?

Download your design files and preview above. Keep the files together: they show your artwork, garment, panel placements, requested size, quantity and notes.

  1. Design review: we need to confirm artwork quality, supported placements, seams, garment size, fabric and color availability.
  2. Personal quote: the final garment, quantity and delivery destination determine the price. A saved draft does not reserve stock or lock a price.
  3. Your approval: review the final mockup, confirmed size and complete price before payment. Production starts only after your design is approved and an order is accepted.

Email your design for review. Send your downloaded design files and preview to midnightdesign107@gmail.com. Saving or downloading keeps the files on your device; attach them and press Send in your email app. A request is for review and pricing, not an accepted order.
