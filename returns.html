Choose with confidence

Sizing & change of mind.

Check the product’s measurements, fit notes and available size chart before choosing your size. Compare those measurements with a garment you already wear.

We do not offer voluntary refunds or exchanges solely because you selected the wrong size or color, or changed your mind. This does not limit any return or withdrawal right you have under applicable law.

Received a different size from the one ordered, or measurements materially outside the listed tolerance? That is a product issue. Contact us with the size label and measurement photos so we can review it.

Something isn’t right

Damage, defects & incorrect items.

If your item arrives damaged, defective, misprinted or different from your order, contact us promptly—preferably within 30 days of delivery—with your order number, a description and clear photos. This reporting preference does not shorten your legal rights.

We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate remedy, such as a replacement or refund, for a verified product or fulfillment error. You will not be charged return or replacement shipping for a confirmed fault.

Please keep the item and packaging while we review it. Don’t send it to an address on the parcel; contact us for any return instructions first. We will tell you if a return is necessary.

Act as soon as possible

Cancellations & order changes.

Contact us immediately with your order number to request cancellation, a size/color change or an address correction. Sending a message does not automatically change or cancel the order.

We can confirm a cancellation and refund when it is still possible to stop production. There is no guaranteed cancellation window; some items cannot be edited after submission. Once production begins, voluntary cancellations may no longer be possible. Your applicable statutory rights still apply.

Saved custom designs and bag items are drafts. You can change or remove them freely because they are not submitted orders.

Delivery problems.

If an estimated delivery date has passed or your parcel appears lost, contact us with the order number and tracking details. Please report a possible loss promptly, preferably within 30 days of the estimated arrival date. We will investigate and arrange the remedy required for a confirmed delivery failure.

Check your delivery address before ordering. If an incorrect address or an unclaimed parcel requires reshipping, we will explain any applicable cost before you agree to it. This does not remove rights arising from our own error.

Rights stay with you

Legal returns & refunds.

This policy does not exclude mandatory consumer protections. Where applicable, including eligible EU distance purchases, you may withdraw within 14 days of delivery. Clearly personalized goods or goods produced to your individual specifications may be exempt. Producing a standard catalog item after ordering does not, by itself, make it personalized.

For an eligible withdrawal, notify us and return the item within 14 days of your notice. You pay return postage unless we agree otherwise or law requires otherwise. Use beyond normal inspection may reduce the refundable value.

Eligible EU withdrawal refunds include standard delivery, are due within 14 days of notice, and may await goods or dispatch evidence. Other mandatory deadlines apply. Approved refunds use your original payment method; bank processing varies.

Personalization does not remove remedies for defects or incorrect goods. Contact us if your local law provides additional rights.
