About this piece

Moonlit Ruins Yoga Leggings. Rose-tinted moons, gothic ruins and ornamental linework wrap the leggings in a dark celestial composition.

Materials & fit: Smooth microfiber leggings with four-way stretch and a raised waistband. US-produced fabric is 78% polyester / 22% elastane, 290 g/m²; EU fabric is 84% polyester / 16% elastane, 230 g/m². Composition and weight may vary by production region and batch. Avoid rough surfaces, which can snag the fabric.

Care: Wash in cold water with similar colors. Avoid bleach and dry-cleaning. Use low heat when ironing and follow the attached care label for drying or any instructions specific to your item.
