About this piece

Stretchy yoga leggings with a raised waistband and smooth microfiber fabric. Four-way stretch supports movement, while all-over artwork completes the look.

Materials

  • 78% polyester, 22% elastane for fulfillment in the US
  • 84% polyester, 16% elastane for fulfillment in EU
  • Fabric weight: 8.56 oz./yd.² (290 g/m²) in the US
  • Fabric weight: 6.78 oz./yd.² (230 g/m²) in EU

Fit

  • Four-way stretch, which means fabric stretches and recovers on the cross and lengthwise grains.
  • Raised waistband

Care

  • Wash in cold water with similar colors.
  • Avoid bleach and dry-cleaning.
  • Use low heat when ironing.
  • Follow the care label for drying instructions.
