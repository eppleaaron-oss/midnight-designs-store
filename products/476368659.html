About this piece

Terror Tapes Athletic T-Shirt. Distressed horror-poster artwork combines a skull, curling smoke and bold red Terror Tapes lettering on black.

Materials & fit: Lightweight, soft sports mesh with four-way stretch and a comfortable fit. Fabric is 92% polyester / 8% spandex in the US or 90% polyester / 10% elastane in the EU, approximately 150 g/m². Overlock and coverstitch construction supports an everyday athletic layer.

Care: Wash in cold water with similar colors. Avoid bleach and dry-cleaning. Use low heat when ironing and follow the attached care label for drying or any instructions specific to your item.
