From the studio to your doorstep

Shipping.

A little patience for your next after-hours essential. Here’s how production and delivery work.

Orders are not open yet. Checkout is currently unavailable. The information below is a planning guide; shipping prices and eligible destinations must be confirmed before we accept orders.

01 / Production

Made to order.

Typical production takes 2–5 business days after an order is accepted. This is separate from shipping time. Custom artwork may need approval before production can begin.

02 / Transit

On the way.

The delivery route depends on the item, production location and destination. Items in one order may travel in separate packages.

03 / Arrival

Your new rotation.

Estimated arrival combines production and transit. Business days exclude weekends and holidays. Estimates are not guaranteed; stock availability, customs and carrier disruptions can add time.

No surprise promises

Shipping costs.

Shipping prices are not configured yet. Bag totals show merchandise only and exclude shipping and taxes. We have not announced a flat rate or free-shipping offer.

The amount will depend on your delivery address, items, quantities and available service. Separate packages can increase the cost. A complete payable total must be available before orders open.

International deliveries may incur customs duties, import taxes or handling fees. Whether these are prepaid or collected on arrival depends on the destination and service.

Destination guide

Where can it go?

Store shipping destinations are being finalized. Our production network supports many countries, including the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Japan and much of Europe. Eligibility depends on each item and the full delivery address.

Unavailable and limited destinations

Current network restrictions exclude Russia, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, Syria, North Korea, Venezuela, the Gaza Strip, and Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions in Ukraine.

Ukraine and Kosovo have limited product availability. Some products cannot enter India because of their country of origin. Temporary carrier restrictions can also apply. This guide does not confirm eligibility for an individual order.

Utility backpack and selected custom items

The utility backpack has a narrower destination list. Some custom footwear, outerwear, track pants, pajamas and bags also have product-specific limits.

Network destinations for these limited items

Austria, Australia, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France (mainland only), Germany, Greece, Vatican City, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, San Marino, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain (excluding Ibiza), Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, United Kingdom and United States.

Excluded: overseas territories, Guam, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands, US Virgin Islands and military bases. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico can have much longer transit; small parcels may take over 30 days.

This is network coverage, not an active store shipping offer. The exact item and address still need confirmation.
